In this questionnaire, you are being asked questions about yourself. There 
are no ‘right’ or ‘wrong’ answers. Your answers should be the ones that 
are ‘right’ for you. 
 
You may ask for help if there is something you do not understand or you are 
not sure how to answer a question.  
 
Your answers will be combined with others to make totals and averages in 
which no individual can be identified. All your answers will be kept 
confidential.
