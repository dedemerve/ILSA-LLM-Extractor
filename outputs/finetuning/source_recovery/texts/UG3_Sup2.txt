Population 3
Overview
This supplement contains documentation of national adaptations of the international versions of the
student and school background questionnaire items. See Chapter 2 of the accompanying User
Guide for a description of the TIMSS background questionnaires. The international versions of the
questionnaires are provided in Supplement 1 of the User Guide.
This documentation provides users with a guide to evaluate the availability of internationally-
comparable data for use in secondary analyses involving the TIMSS contextual variables. The
documentation of national adaptations is presented in two sections corresponding to each of the
Population 3 questionnaire types:
Section 1:
Student Background Questionnaire
Section 2:
School Background Questionnaire
Each section contains specific national documentation about individual background items in order
of questionnaire item location. Documentation is included for all variables for which there are some
countries: (i) included in the database but the version of the question administered is country-
specific or adapted from the international version or (ii) not included in the database. For each item,
the following information is provided:
• 
Questionnaire Item Location Reference (as presented in Table 7.4 of the User Guide)
• 
Question (an abbreviated version of the main question with any relevant subitems and
general notes)
• 
Variable name(s) for each questionnaire item or subitem
• 
Table of National Adaptation Documentation (listed by country and by subitem)
The version of the question shown in the documentation in this supplement is a shortened version
included to communicate the basic information about what was asked in each item and to aid in the
interpretation of the specific documentation comments. For the exact form of each question, see
Supplement 1.
The Table of National Adaptation Documentation for each item lists any countries for which
internationally-comparable data for the indicated item or subitems are unavailable or for which data
are included with documentation regarding country-specific questions or questions with national
adaptations of the international versions. It should be noted that this documentation does not
include any cases where simple recodes of the national data were made to produce internationally-
comparable data in the international variables (e.g. option reversals). In the Tables of National
Adaptation Documentation, countries are given one of two different data codes with the following
definitions:

Code D:  National data for country are included in the international variable.
This code is used for items where the international version asks for country-specific
questions or nationally-adapted options and where modified national versions included for
some countries are considered appropriate for comparison of univariate statistics. For these
items, some brief documentation is included to describe the national versions of the
questions, to assist the user in data interpretation.
Code X:  National data for country are NOT included in the international
variable.
This code is used to refer to all items that were (i) not administered, (ii) not applicable, or
(iii) deleted for a variety of reasons (e.g. not internationally comparable, removed per NRC
request, or removed due to other data problems). A general comment is included for the
majority of these cases: ‘Question Not Administered or Data Not Internationally
Comparable’. Other comments may be included for some variables where some
description is required to explain the absence of data.
The documentation provided is as complete as possible given the information provided by the
National Research Coordinators (NRCs) and the resources available for reviewing the individual
national adaptations. The review of national adaptations was instituted for the majority of the
background questionnaire items and, in particular, for any of the items that were used for reporting
the contextual data in the international reports.

General Documentation Issues for Student Questionnaire Items
Czech Republic:  Two versions of the student questionnaire were administered:  G version
administered to students in Gymnasia (academic schools) and V version administered to students
in technical and vocational schools. Students administered the two different types of questionnaires
may be identified by IDSTRAT second digit:  1 = V version; 4 = G version.  For most questions,
the two versions of the questionnaire are the same.  Any differences between the two versions are
documented in the Summary of National Adaptations.
Germany:  Four versions of the student questionnaire were administered:  G version administered
to students in gymnasia (GS) or comprehensive schools (IGS); FG version administered to
students in higher technical professional schools (Fachgymnasia - FG); BFS/FOS version
administered to students in full-time vocational (BFS) or applied science (FOS) schools; and BS
version administered to students in apprenticeship programs (BS).  Questionnaire numbers SQ3-1
through SQ3-11/1-2 were the same for all versions of the questionnaires; question SQ3-12/1-4
was not administered in the BS version, and questions SQ3-13A1-D4 and up were not
administered in either vocational version of the questionnaire (BFS/FOS or BS). These differences
resulting in high percentages of students coded as ‘not administered’ are reflected in the Summary
of National Adaptations.
General Documentation Issues for School Questionnaire Items
Czech Republic:  Two versions of the school questionnaire were administered:  G version
administered to gymnasia (academic schools) and V version administered to technical and
vocational schools. For most questions, the two versions of the questionnaire are the same.  For
some school background variables the questions are different for the two versions and combined
data from the G and V versions are included.  Separate data for the G and V schools may be
obtained by filtering by the IDSTRAT variable.  The second digit of IDSTRAT identifies the G and
V school types: 1 = V version; 4 = G version.  These types of items with different data for G and
V schools are documented in the Summary of National Adaptations.
Germany:  Two versions of the student questionnaire were administered:  G version administered
to gymnasia (GS), and comprehensive schools (IGS); V version administered to higher technical
professional schools (FG), applied science schools (FOS), full-time vocational schools (BFS), or
apprenticeship (BS) schools.  Some questions were administered only in the G version of the
questionnaire, and the vocational schools are coded as ‘not administered’ for these items.  These
items are indicated in the Summary of National Adaptations.
Netherlands:  The school questionnaire was not administered.  The school background data for
the Netherlands will reflect 100% ‘not administered’ for all background questionnaire items.
