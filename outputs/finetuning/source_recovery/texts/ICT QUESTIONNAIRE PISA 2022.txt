The following questions will ask you about digital resources that you may use, both 
inside and outside of school. Digital resources refer here to the following: 
• digital devices or “hardware” (e.g., computers, tablets, smartphones, 3D 
printers) 
• software (e.g., programs, Apps, communication tools, educational learning 
tools); and  
• online resources (e.g., websites, web portals).  
 
Please think of all of these when answering the questions on the following screens.
